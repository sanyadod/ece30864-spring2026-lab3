Pros of Agile Development
1. Agile is flexible because changes can be made during development instead of being fixed at the beginning.
2. It allows for frequent feedback, which helps teams fix problems early and improve the product.

Cons of Agile Development
1. Agile can be less predictable since the scope and timeline may change throughout the project.
2. It requires a lot of communication and teamwork, which can be difficult to manage.
