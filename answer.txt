Question 1
Pros of Agile Development
1. Agile is flexible because changes can be made during development instead of being fixed at the beginning.
2. It allows for frequent feedback, which helps teams fix problems early and improve the product.

Cons of Agile Development
1. Agile can be less predictable since the scope and timeline may change throughout the project.
2. It requires a lot of communication and teamwork, which can be difficult to manage.

Question 2
Scrum Master - The Scrum Master makes sure the team follows the Scrum process and Agile practices. 
They help remove obstacles that slow the team down and make sure meetings like stand-ups and sprint reviews happen smoothly.

Product Owner - The Product Owner decides what features are most important and manages the product backlog. 
They represent the customer’s needs and make sure the team is building the right thing.

Question 3
Sprint Planning 
1. The team discusses which tasks or stories will be worked on in the sprint.
2. They decide the sprint goal and estimate how much work each task will take.

Daily Stand-Up
1. Each team member shares what they worked on yesterday.
2. They talk about what they will work on today and mention any blockers.

Sprint Review / Retrospective
1. The team shows what they completed during the sprint and gets feedback.
2. They discuss what went well, what didn’t go well, and how to improve next sprint.

Question 4
Epic - A large piece of work that cannot be completed in one sprint.
It is broken down into multiple smaller stories or tasks.

Story - A feature or requirement written from the user’s point of view.
It can usually be completed within one sprint.

Task - A specific piece of work that needs to be done.
Usually technical work that supports a story or epic.

Bug - A problem or error in the system that needs to be fixed.
It is created when something does not work as expected.
